Our story

Gunita Kopi opened in April 2025 along Reparo Road in Caloocan, founded by Mr. Eric Espedilion. Gunita means memory and Kopi means coffee.

We serve students, teachers, workers, and neighbors who want a warm, familiar place to talk, study, and unwind.

Mission

To provide quality coffee, meals, and service that create comfort, happiness, and meaningful experiences for customers.

Vision

To become a well-known local café recognized for its warm atmosphere, friendly service, and positive community impact.
